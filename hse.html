Quality, health, safety & environment

QHSE Policy

Evomec Global Services Limited (EGSL) is a leader in engineering, procurement and construction of oil and gas pipelines, process facilities, civil works, heavy equipment leasing and maintenance.

Certified systems

Compliance & Certifications

Evomec’s QHSE and quality systems are independently audited and certified. Certificates are available on request.

Our commitment

We are strongly committed to ensuring a safe and healthy working environment for our workers, contractors, visitors and others working at our worksites or who may be affected by our operations — as well as protecting the environment.

To achieve these goals, all Company locations shall adhere to the following principles.

Identification and evaluation of all QHSE hazards or aspects and the management of those risks to reduce their impacts to acceptable levels.

Compliance with all applicable QHSE legislation.

Prevention of incidents, injuries, and pollution.

Reduction of waste and conservation of resources.

Recording and communicating QHSE performance throughout the organization.

Continual improvement of QHSE performance.

The right to STOP WORK

Every worker has the right to stop work and intervene when it is not safe to continue.

Governance & implementation

Leadership commitment

The Managing Director and senior management shall visibly uphold these principles throughout the Company and integrate them into the Company’s QHSE Management System. Management shall regularly review QHSE performance.

Management responsibility

Management and supervisory personnel at each division and location shall be responsible for implementing and maintaining the Company’s QHSE Policy and Management System.

Contractors & subcontractors

Contractors and subcontractors shall work in accordance with the Company’s QHSE Policy and comply with applicable QHSE legislation. Regular QHSE audits shall be conducted to evaluate conformance to the QHSE Management System.

Training & accountability

Every employee whose work may create a significant QHSE impact shall be trained and held accountable for complying with the Company’s QHSE Policy and related procedures, practices, instructions and rules. Working safely and in an environmentally appropriate manner are conditions of employment.
